About

I build the part of a product that people touch. That means clean, well-organised code, layouts that hold up on any phone, and interfaces that stay usable with a keyboard or a screen reader.

I care about page speed, readable code and small details like focus states and empty states. I enjoy working with designers and backend engineers, and I like taking a project from the first sketch to a live site.

I am a strong team player and open to new ideas and innovation. I am also very good with AI, and I use it every day to build, debug and learn faster.

Beyond code, I do graphic design in Canva, edit video in CapCut, make PowerPoint slides and work on Excel sheets, so I can take care of the visuals and the paperwork around a project too.
